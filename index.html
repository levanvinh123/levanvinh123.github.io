Welcome!

This site started as a high school project while I was first diving into HTML and CSS, and I honestly didn't put much time into updating the projects or information since then. I'm keeping it that way on purpose instead of scrapping it, there's something worth keeping in seeing where I started.

A little bit about myself. My name is Vinh. Back then I was still figuring things out, curious about a lot of things but without a clear direction yet. Near the end of 12th grade, that changed. I found a definite goal, meaning, and value that I wanted to strive for. Now I'm an incoming honors mechanical engineering student at Iowa State, a research assistant at Ames National Lab (one of 17 national labs in the US), and I've got three months of experience as a motorbike mechanic intern under my belt.

Looking back at this old site makes me a little nostalgic, and it gives me a firmer will and dedication going forward. It's partial proof to my past self that as long as I put in the effort, want something enough, and set my goals carefully, I can drastically improve my capability and get closer to where I want to be. This is just the beginning of the journey, and I'll keep learning and improving from here.

Engineering is my full-time hobby now, mechatronics especially, but honestly I love science and good engineering in any field. Design and simulation, smart manufacturing, process optimization, and systems engineering all interest me a lot too. I'd still like to pick up economics and finance once I have the time, and I'm still genuinely into psychology, philosophy, and staying healthy along the way.

On this site, I'm just going to share some of the projects I've built and articles/websites I've found interesting. If any of it ends up being helpful or even just a little bit intriguing to you, then that'd be great!

My Journey

2023

I won first prize in the provincial math competition and got into a gifted high school off the back of it. Most of that year was split between math and starting my IELTS prep, the first real signs of a direction forming.

Grade 9 Mathematics

2024

A year of trying a bit of everything - art, music, media, editing, sales, science fields outside physics and math - mostly to figure out what I enjoy doing. I also finished my IELTS prep and landed a 7.5.

Grade 10 7.5 IELTS

2025

A big year. I spent months researching and reflecting before officially deciding to pursue mechanical engineering. A lot of that choice came from the genuine joy I get when tinkering with my own projects. I built my first IR remote project on an Arduino Nano, took the SAT and hit a 1550, then spent the end of the year working through university applications.

Grade 11 1550 SAT Engineering & Uni apps

2026

Easily the busiest year yet. I joined a robotics team for First Tech Challenge, designing the intake subsystem, and we placed 2nd out of 40+ teams. I interned at a motorbike repair shop, getting real hands-on time with CVT and engine systems under mechanics with decades of experience. On the side, I designed and built a quadcopter drone from salvaged parts for under $120, handling everything myself from part sourcing to firmware to flight tuning. Now I'm at Iowa State as an honors mechanical engineering student, working as a research assistant at Ames National Lab and designing a low-voltage battery pack for the school's Formula SAE team.
